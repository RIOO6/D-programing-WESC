First Aid and Nursing Fundamentals

Develop calm, practical first-response skills for everyday emergencies, patient support, and safe care routines.

Duration6 weeks
FormatHands-on lab
OutcomeCertificate

What you will learn

Practice the habits that make a difference before professional help arrives. The course combines demonstrations, scenario work, and instructor feedback in a supportive learning environment.

  1. Safety and patient assessmentSecure a scene, assess a person, and communicate clearly during an emergency.
  2. Basic first aid responseRespond to common injuries, bleeding, burns, choking, and sudden illness.
  3. CPR awareness and practiceUnderstand the response sequence and practice appropriate actions using training equipment.
  4. Patient care fundamentalsExplore hygiene, comfort, observation, documentation, and respectful patient support.
  5. Scenario assessmentComplete guided emergency scenarios and receive feedback on technique and communication.
