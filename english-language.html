English Language Program

Build practical English confidence for study, work, and everyday communication through guided conversation and useful writing practice.

Duration10 weeks
FormatLive practice
OutcomeProgress review

What you will learn

Move from memorizing language to using it. Lessons center on real situations, supportive feedback, and the vocabulary you need to communicate with more ease.

  1. Everyday conversationIntroduce yourself, ask questions, clarify meaning, and keep a conversation moving.
  2. Workplace communicationPractice meetings, messages, presentations, and professional email patterns.
  3. Grammar in contextStrengthen sentence structure and accuracy through useful, repeatable examples.
  4. Reading and listeningBuild strategies for understanding articles, instructions, short talks, and interviews.
  5. Final communication projectComplete a practical speaking and writing task with personalized feedback.
